KindPulse is a charitable token project designed to support children facing serious illnesses while using blockchain technology to make project activity and donations easier to verify.

Our Mission Tokenomics

Our Mission

Technology is the tool. Helping children is the purpose.

Supporting Children Facing Serious Illness

KindPulse is being developed with a clear charitable mission: to help support children affected by serious and special illnesses. The project aims to create a transparent framework through which community participation and project resources can contribute to meaningful charitable causes.


Initial and future charitable recipients may include established humanitarian organizations, hospitals, foundations, and other verified organizations serving children in need.

How KindPulse Works

A simple model connecting blockchain participation with charitable impact.

01 · Community

A community can participate in the KindPulse ecosystem through $KPUN and support the project's charitable mission.

02 · Transparency

Blockchain transactions can provide a public and independently verifiable record of on-chain movements and project activity.

03 · Charity

Project resources designated for charitable purposes are intended to support verified organizations and causes serving children.

Release & Burn Schedule

Controlled supply expansion followed by planned supply reduction.

Launch
20% Initial Release

200,000,000 KPUN are scheduled for initial release.

Year 2
10% Additional Release

A further 100,000,000 KPUN are scheduled for release, subject to the final launch documentation.

Year 3
Planned Supply Reduction

No new scheduled release. A planned burn of 8% of the original 1 billion supply equals 80,000,000 KPUN.

Long Term
Controlled Releases & Burns

Future releases and burns are intended to follow a clearly published schedule, with burns taken from tokens that have not yet been released whenever technically and contractually possible.

Transparency

Trust should be supported by verifiable information.

On-Chain Records
Relevant blockchain transactions can be independently inspected through the Solana network.
Public Wallets
Designated project and charitable wallets are intended to be publicly disclosed when they are established and secured.
Multisignature Protection
The planned charitable reserve is intended to use a multisignature wallet so that charitable funds are not controlled by a single person.
Charitable Reporting
Future charitable distributions should be accompanied by appropriate transaction records and recipient information whenever disclosure is legally and operationally possible.

Community Governance

Governance is planned as the project develops.

KindPulse may introduce community participation in future decisions concerning charitable priorities and ecosystem development. Any governance mechanism will be publicly documented before it is presented as an active feature of the project.
