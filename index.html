01 · Mission

Helping children is the purpose.

Blockchain is the tool. The charitable mission comes first.

Supporting Children Facing Serious or Complex Illnesses

KindPulse is being developed with a clear charitable mission: to support children affected by serious or complex illnesses. The project aims to create a transparent framework through which community participation and project resources can contribute to meaningful humanitarian causes.


Initial and future charitable recipients may include established humanitarian organizations, hospitals, foundations, and other verified organizations serving children in need.


A

Community

A community can participate in the KindPulse ecosystem through $KPUN and support the project's charitable mission.

B

Transparency

Blockchain transactions can provide public and independently verifiable records of on-chain movements and project activity.

C

Charity

Resources designated for charitable purposes are intended to support verified organizations and causes serving children.

03 · Tokenomics

12-Year Supply Model

A controlled release and burn structure designed around a 1 billion KPUN initial supply, with planned burns coming from tokens reserved for future release.

1B Initial Supply
640M Planned Total Release
360M Planned Total Burn
12 Years Planned Schedule

Controlled Release

Tokens are planned to enter circulation gradually rather than releasing the entire initial supply at launch.

Planned Burns

Planned burns are intended to come from tokens reserved for future release, rather than from tokens already held by users, subject to final contract design.

06 · Transparency

Built for verifiability.

Trust should be supported by information that can be independently checked whenever technically and legally possible.

On-Chain Records

Relevant blockchain transactions can be independently inspected through the Solana network explorer once deployed.

Multisignature Protection

KindPulse intends to use a multisignature wallet for the charitable reserve so that funds are not controlled by a single individual.

Planned Charity Reserve

The planned charitable reserve is dedicated to supporting verified hospitals, foundations, and international organizations serving children in need.
