FAQ

What makes Solana Token-2022 useful for KindPulse?

Token-2022 provides additional extensions and programmable capabilities that may be useful for the KindPulse ecosystem. The specific extensions used by KindPulse will be disclosed as the token architecture is finalized.

How are medical recipients verified?

The project intends to work with established hospitals, accredited charitable organizations and registered medical organizations to help ensure that charitable support reaches children in need.

Are the planned burns executed automatically?

Planned burns are intended to come from unreleased reserves according to the 15-year model. The exact on-chain mechanism will be defined during final Token-2022 contract design and security review.

Can the charity reserve support different organizations?

Yes. The planned reserve is intentionally not tied permanently to one organization. Support may be directed to verified hospitals, charities, humanitarian organizations or international organizations.

Is $KPUN an investment with guaranteed returns?

No. $KPUN should not be presented as a guaranteed investment, profit or return. Digital assets involve significant risks.
