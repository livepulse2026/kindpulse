Executive Whitepaper

1. Vision & Problem Statement

Traditional philanthropic institutions are frequently compromised by excessive administrative overhead, lack of financial clarity, and geo-restricted capital flows. KindPulse ($KPUN) addresses these structural flaws by merging high-throughput blockchain infrastructure with a verified on-chain donation loop.

2. Custody & Multi-Sig Governance

To eliminate single-point-of-failure risks, all reserve vaults operate strictly under a Squads v4 Multi-Signature Contract on Solana.

  • Consensus Threshold: Requires 2-of-3 multi-sig authentication before any transfer or program alteration can execute.
  • Auditable Operations: All proposed payouts and signer actions are broadcast publicly prior to execution.

3. Anti-Dump Airdrop Framework

To prevent bot exploitation and market dumping, the 10% Airdrop allocation enforces a multi-tiered Proof-of-Humanity distribution strategy:

  • Sybil-Proof Tasks: Engagement with verified community initiatives and on-chain verification.
  • Proof-of-Donation Multiplier: Dynamic bonuses for early supporters of micro-philanthropy campaigns.
  • Vesting Schedule: 25% unlocked at TGE, 75% linearly over 3 months.

4. Ecosystem Charter

KINDPULSE ($KPUN) PROTOCOL CHARTER

1. BINDING PRINCIPLES: All Charity, Team, and Dev reserves MUST reside inside the designated Squads v4 Multi-Sig Vault.
2. CHARITY INTEGRITY: The 40% Charity Reserve is strictly non-custodial and governed by on-chain community proposals.
3. TRANSPARENCY: All multi-sig addresses, liquidity locks, and burn receipts will remain permanently public.
