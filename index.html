01 · Mission

Helping children is the purpose.

Blockchain is the tool. The charitable mission comes first.

Supporting Children Facing Serious Illness

KindPulse is being developed with a clear charitable mission: to help support children affected by serious and special illnesses. The project aims to create a transparent framework through which community participation and project resources can contribute to meaningful charitable causes.


Initial and future charitable recipients may include established humanitarian organizations, hospitals, foundations, and other verified organizations serving children in need.

06 · Transparency

Built for verifiability.

Trust should be supported by information that can be independently checked whenever technically and legally possible.

On-Chain Records

Relevant blockchain transactions can be independently inspected through the Solana network explorer.

Multisignature Protection

The planned charitable reserve uses a multisignature wallet so that funds are never controlled by a single individual.


Planned Charity Reserve & Public Auditing

KindPulse intends to establish a dedicated charitable reserve protected by multisignature authorization. The reserve is not intended to be permanently tied to a single charity or organization. Depending on circumstances, charitable support may be directed to verified hospitals, foundations, humanitarian organizations, or international organizations serving children.

07 · FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to standard questions about our mission and technical mechanics.

What makes Solana Token-2022 ideal for KindPulse?

Token-2022 allows for enhanced programmable extensions like automated charity allocations, built-in transfer fee configurations, and enhanced transaction metadata for audit compliance.

How are medical recipients verified?

We work exclusively alongside established hospitals, accredited charitable NGOs, and registered medical organizations to guarantee that support reaches children directly.

Are the planned burns executed automatically?

Burns are intended to be executed periodically from unreleased reserves as per the 15-year roadmap, maintaining maximum scarcity and transparency.
